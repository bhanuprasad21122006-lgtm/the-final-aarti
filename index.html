The 75th Sarvajanik Ganeshotsav

You are Aarav, a dedicated volunteer helping organize the city's grandest Ganesh festival.

The sacred Final Aarti is set for precisely 6:30 PM. Devotees from all over are gathering, but unforeseen crises loom:

  • ⚡ Critical electrical and sound equipment hazards
  • 🥥 Incomplete preparation of 108 sacred Ukadiche Modaks
  • 🛡️ Blocked queue routes and crowd bottlenecks
  • 🌺 Missing sacred marigold garlands and unlit deepstambhs

The Sacred Time Loop: If disaster strikes or the festival is unprepared at 6:30 PM, time resets to 5:55 PM. But you keep all recorded memories in your diary!

Objective: Reach 100% Festival Readiness before 6:30 PM to save the Final Aarti!

How to Play

Festival Gameplay Loop

1

Explore & Talk [E]

Walk around the courtyard. Talk to organizers, cooks, electricians, and devotees using [E] to uncover what needs fixing.

2

Watch the Clock (5:55 PM → 6:30 PM)

Time moves forward in real-time. Key events happen at set hours: rain arrives at 6:08 PM, modaks must steam by 6:10 PM, and Aarti begins at 6:30 PM.

3

Check Your Diary [Tab]

Every clue, person, and timetable event you discover is permanently logged in your Volunteer Diary [Tab] and persists across time loops.

4

Intervene & Prepare Offerings

Solve problems before catastrophes hit. Protect the wiring, guide devotees, clear cargo bottlenecks, and light sacred lamps.

5

Rewind Time [R]

If power fails or preparation falls short, rewind time with [R]. Armed with prior loop discoveries, execute the perfect sequence!

6

Achieve 100% Readiness

Reach 100% readiness to unlock the sacred 6:30 PM celebration with divine rays, bells, flower showers, and celebration!
